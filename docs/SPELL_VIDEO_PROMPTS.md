# Prompt video per gli incantesimi di Spellcaster (Waving Hands)

Questo documento raccoglie prompt pronti all’uso per generare **clip video brevi (3–6 secondi)** di ogni gesto / incantesimo di Spellcaster.  
Sono pensati per modelli di generazione video (text-to-video): atmosfera fantasy da duello di maghi, inquadratura cinematografica, **solo effetti sonori** — niente voce, dialogo o narrazione.

Uso tipico: copia il blocco di un singolo incantesimo (eventualmente preceduto dal *common prefix*), oppure concatena il prefix al prompt dello spell.

---

## Prefisso comune (opzionale, inglese)

Incolla questo prefisso **prima** del prompt specifico dello spell, se il modello risponde meglio a istruzioni di stile ripetute:

```
Cinematic fantasy duel clip, 3–6 seconds, photorealistic with subtle magical stylization, shallow depth of field, dramatic lighting, no subtitles, no on-screen text, no logos. Silent dialogue: absolutely no speech, no voiceover, no narration, no whispered words. Audio: immersive sound effects and ambient magic only. Camera: smooth, intentional motion. Subject: a solitary battle mage in ritual robes casting with precise hand gestures.
```

## Protezione (Protection)

### Shield — `P`

```
3–4 second cinematic close-up: a battle mage raises one open palm toward camera. A translucent hexagonal energy shield blooms outward from the hand in a soft blue-silver flare, locking into a curved barrier that ripples once then holds. Sparks glance harmlessly off the surface. Camera pushes in slowly on the glowing palm and shield edge. SFX only: crystalline chime as the ward forms, soft energy hum, faint metallic ping of deflected impact. No speech, no narration.
```

### Remove enchantment — `P-D-W-P`

```
4–5 second cinematic medium shot: mage completes a four-gesture sequence ending on an open palm. Dark violet runes and ghostly chains wrapped around a target figure shatter like glass, dissolving into ash and light. Residual curse wisps are pulled away and vanish. Camera orbits slightly as enchantment debris scatters. SFX only: cracking glass, unraveling magical threads, a clean whoosh as curses break, fading dissonant tones. No speech, no narration.
```

### Magic mirror — `C-(w`

```
4–5 second cinematic shot: mage claps once, then both hands wave in unison. A floating oval of liquid mercury-like magic appears before them, reflecting the battlefield in distorted silver. An incoming spell-bolt hits the mirror and ricochets back along the same path in a bright streak. Camera catches the clap, the mirror birth, and the reflected beam. SFX only: sharp clap, liquid metal shimmer, high-pitched reflection ping, swooping rebound whoosh. No speech, no narration.
```

### Counter-spell — `W-P-P` or `W-W-S`

```
4–5 second cinematic clip: mage waves, then seals with palms (or snaps). A hostile spell mid-flight is caught in a lattice of pale gold glyphs that collapse inward and snuff the spell out like a candle. Brief shield-like shimmer on the final gesture. Camera tracks the cancelled projectile dissolving into harmless motes. SFX only: glyph lock-in chimes, compressed magical silence thud, soft extinguish hiss, residual sparkle. No speech, no narration.
```

### Dispel magic — `C-D-P-W`

```
5–6 second wide cinematic shot: mage claps, points, palm, then waves. A concentric shockwave of white-blue antimagic expands across the arena, stripping floating enchantments, collapsing summoned silhouettes into smoke, and blanking glowing runes on stone and cloth. Dust and light settle. Camera follows the wave outward then settles on cleared air. SFX only: thunderous clap, expanding bass whoomp, cascading shatter of many small wards, wind rush into emptiness. No speech, no narration.
```

### Raise dead — `D-W-W-F-W-C`

```
5–6 second cinematic low-angle shot: mage completes a long gesture chain ending in a clap. Pale green-gold life energy descends into a fallen body; eyes open, wounds seal with soft light, the figure sits up gasping silently as dust falls away. Camera rises with the resurrected form. SFX only: deep heartbeat returning, bone-and-fabric rustle, rising choir-like tonal swell (non-verbal), clap punctuation, soft inhale of breath as sound effect only—no words. No speech, no narration.
```

### Cure light wounds — `D-F-W`

```
3–4 second intimate cinematic close-up: mage points, fingers flex, then waves over a shallow cut on an arm. Soft emerald light stitches the wound closed; a single drop of blood lifts and evaporates. Skin looks whole. Gentle handheld feel. SFX only: delicate chime, liquid healing drip, soft tissue seal, faint breeze. No speech, no narration.
```

### Cure heavy wounds — `D-F-P-W`

```
4–5 second cinematic medium close-up: mage points, fingers, palm, wave over a deeper injury. Stronger green-gold radiance floods the wound; torn flesh knits in visible layers, a dark disease-like stain on the skin burns away into clean light. Camera holds on the healed area. SFX only: deeper healing chord, flesh knitting rustle, purifying crackle as illness leaves, warm fade-out hum. No speech, no narration.
```

## Danno (Damage)

### Missile — `S-D`

```
3–4 second cinematic tracking shot: in a stone dueling courtyard at dusk, a battle mage snaps fingers then jabs a pointed finger forward. A compact cobalt-white kinetic missile forms at the fingertip, corkscrews once, and streaks in a straight glowing trail into the rival’s chest with a sharp impact burst and flying grit. Shallow depth of field, motion blur on the bolt. Camera rides beside the missile then cuts to impact. SFX only: crisp finger snap, whistling projectile Doppler, hard impact thud, spark shower, stone chip ricochets. No speech, no narration, no on-screen text.
```

### Finger of death — `P-W-P-F-S-S-S-D`

```
5–6 second dark cinematic climax: mage finishes a long ritual with three snaps and a pointed finger. A black-green necrotic beam leaps from the fingertip; the target’s body stiffens, life-light extinguishes in the eyes, silhouette crumples as ash-like motes lift from the skin. Extreme slow push-in on the final point. SFX only: rising dread drone, triple snaps like bones, death-beam scream of air (non-vocal), silence drop, body collapse. No speech, no narration.
```

### Lightning bolt — `D-F-F-D-D` (long) / `W-D-D-C` (short)

```
4–5 second high-energy cinematic clip (prefer long form): mage points, double finger flourish, then double pointed jab. Ozone haze gathers; a blinding white-blue lightning bolt explodes from the final jab, arcs across the duel field in branching forks, and detonates on the target with a white flash and settling smoke. Alternate short form: wave, double point, clap that triggers the same bolt. Camera whip-pans along the bolt path, brief lens flare. SFX only: crackling charge-up, deafening thunder crack, electrical sizzle, after-echo rumble; clap only if short form. No speech, no narration, no on-screen text.
```

## Opzionali (Non-incantesimi)

### Stab

```
3–4 second brutal close cinematic: the mage forgoes casting and drives a single dagger forward in a sharp thrust at monster or rival wizard. Steel flash, impact, brief blood or creature ichor; not mirrored or flashy—raw physical violence in a magic duel. Camera snap-zoom on the strike. SFX only: blade draw whisper, cloth pierce, impact thud, short pain cry as SFX only (no words). No speech, no narration.
```

### Surrender — `(p`

```
3–4 second somber cinematic: both of the mage’s hands open simultaneously in empty palms held out—universal surrender. Magical auras gutter and die; the duel field’s tension collapses. Opponent remains poised. Camera pulls back from the open hands to the whole defeated stance. SFX only: dual soft palm sound, magic powering down, wind across an empty arena, distant crowdless silence. No speech, no narration.
```

## Note

- Durata consigliata: **3–6 secondi** per clip; i prompt più lunghi (raise dead, storm, finger of death, permanency) tendono al limite alto.
- **Audio:** solo SFX / ambience. Evitare musica con testo; se il modello aggiunge voci, rinforzare nel prompt: `no speech, no dialogue, no narration, SFX only`.
- **Gesti:** le sigle seguono `docs/RULES.md` (`F` dita, `P` palmo, `S` snap, `W` onda, `D` dito puntato, `C` clap; `(x` = stesso gesto con entrambe le mani).
- **Lightning bolt:** un solo prompt copre la variante lunga (`D-F-F-D-D`) e corta (`W-D-D-C`); specifica nel prompt quale sequenza vuoi visualizzare se serve fedeltà gestuale.
- **Summon Elemental:** stesso pattern `C-S-W-W-S`; usa i due prompt (fuoco / ghiaccio) come scelte estetiche separate.
- Stile coerente: mantieni lo stesso mage, palette e arena tra le clip se stai costruendo un set; riutilizza il prefisso comune.
- Riferimento regole: [RULES.md](./RULES.md).
